Q8: CPI formula
EXPECTED: CPI = sum of (credits × marks) divided by sum of credits, over the courses passed with at least 50%
RETRIEVAL HIT: True   RETRIEVED: ['conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf p9 #manual_0', 'conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf p9 #23', 'conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf p8 #20']
ANSWER: The formula for calculating the Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) is: CPI = (sum of Cn x Mn) divided by (sum of Cn), where the sums are taken only over the courses that the student has passed with not less than 50% marks and for which credits are earned. Cn is the number of credits earned for course n in any semester. Mn is the marks obtained by the student in course n. N is the total number of courses over which the performance is being measured, and n runs from 1 to N. All courses have maximum marks of 100 irrespective of the number of credits assigned to the course. Courses in which the student appeared but secured less than 50% marks are not counted; they are treated as Audit Courses and shown that way in the final mark sheet.

Source: conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf, Page: 9

Q9: What is the difference between the penalty for possessing notes and using them ?
EXPECTED: Possessing notes without using them or helping anyone: cancellation of the entire examination. Using the material or helping another candidate: cancellation of the entire examination taken during the year, plus debarment from any University examination for 12 months
RETRIEVAL HIT: True   RETRIEVED: ['regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_1_0', 'regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_2_0', 'regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_5_2']
ANSWER: Based on the provided documents, the differences in penalties depend on whether the candidate merely possessed the material or made actual use of it:

Case 1: Candidate is found in possession of items (such as mobile phone, papers, books, notes, written notes on question paper, clothes, body, table, desk, ruler, or instruments) that could be helpful in answering the paper, but has *not* attempted to take any assistance herself or give assistance to any other candidate from such material.
Penalty: Cancellation of the Entire Examination.

Case 2: Candidate is found in possession of items (such as mobile phone, papers, books, notes, written notes on question paper, clothes, body, table, desk, foot rule, or instruments) that could be helpful in answering the paper, and has made *actual use* of the material herself or given assistance thereof to any other candidate during the examination.
Penalty: Cancellation of the Entire Examination taken by the Candidate during the year and further debarring him from appearing at any Examination of the University within a span of one year (12 months).

Source: regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf, Page: 5-8
