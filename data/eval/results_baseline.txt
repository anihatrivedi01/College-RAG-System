Q2: What is the punishment for carrying a mobile phone into the exam?
EXPECTED: Possession only: cancellation of the entire examination. If the phone was used or help was given: cancellation of the entire examination taken during the year plus debarment for 12 months
RETRIEVAL HIT: True   RETRIEVED: ['regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p2 #4', 'regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_2_0', 'regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_1_0']
ANSWER: Based on the provided documents, the punishment for carrying a mobile phone into the exam depends on whether the candidate made actual use of the material:

* **Case 1:** If the candidate is found in possession of a mobile phone and has made actual use of the material herself (or given assistance to any other candidate during the examination), the punishment is the cancellation of the entire examination taken by the candidate during the year, and further debarring him/her from appearing at any examination of the University within a span of one year (12 months).
* **Case 2:** If the candidate is found in possession of a mobile phone, but has not attempted to take any assistance herself or give assistance to any other candidate from such material, the punishment is the cancellation of the entire examination.

Q8: What is the formula for calculating Cummulative Performance Index?
EXPECTED: CPI = sum of (credits × marks) divided by sum of credits, over the courses passed with at least 50%
RETRIEVAL HIT: True   RETRIEVED: ['conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf p9 #manual_0', 'conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf p9 #23', 'conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf p9 #22']
ANSWER: The formula for calculating the Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) is:
CPI = (sum of Cn x Mn over all courses n) divided by (sum of Cn over all courses n), where n runs from 1 to N. 
(Here, Cn is the number of credits earned for course n in any semester, Mn is the marks obtained by the student in course n, and N is the total number of courses over which the performance is being measured.)

Source: conduct_and_evaluation_of_examinations.pdf, Page: 9

Q9: What is the punishment for impersonation in the exams?
EXPECTED: Cancellation of the entire examination taken by the candidate and further debarring him at any examination of the board during the span of three years(36 months).
RETRIEVAL HIT: True   RETRIEVED: ['regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_4_2', 'regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5 #11', 'regulation_for_unfair_means.pdf p5-8 #table_unfair_means_table_4_1']
ANSWER: Based on the provided documents, the punishments for impersonation are as follows:

- **Case 1 (For a candidate who has impersonated at the examination):** Cancellation of the entire examination taken by the candidate, and further debarring the candidate from any examination of the Board for a span of three years (36 months).
